PORTFOLIO / MAKE THE WORK REVIEWABLE

Show what
you can build.

Start with one project. Document the decisions.

01

A clean-data pipeline

Inputs, validation checks and a reproducible output.

02

A measured classifier

A baseline, held-out results and known failure cases.

03

A small image classifier

A training loop, saved weights and inspected errors.

04

A cited document assistant

Source passages, test questions and honest uncertainty.

05

A tool-using assistant

A narrow task, an approval step and an activity log.

Then deploy project 4 or 5. Add tests, a README, evaluation results and a short runbook.
