Now let’s apply the same idea to a task list, where the goal is to decide what happens next and make delegation easier. Next, we’ll build a transparent revenue model, change the assumptions, and check the calculations with numbers we can verify ourselves. Our fourth example is a packaging concept, where a useful interface helps us explore ideas and prepare a clear review, without pretending the result is ready for manufacturing. Finally, we’ll look at how to turn these small tools into a repeatable way of working, including idea selection and a presentation handoff. Pick one task you repeat every week, give it a clear input and a useful output, and test the smallest version first.
