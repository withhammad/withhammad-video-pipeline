# Independent next-video research — 20 October 2026

**Recommended next: turn a generic AI website into a polished business landing page.** It has a clear visual payoff, matches Hammad's practical AI audience, and recent examples span three independent creators. This is a proposed original demonstration, not a claim that Hammad has already produced these results.

Research: five bounded public YouTube Data API searches, videos published in the previous 30 days, checked 20 October 2026 at 12:44–12:46 UTC. The short-duration filter includes videos under four minutes; these are not all confirmed Shorts. Counts below are public lifetime views at check time, not views per hour, retention, organic reach or channel-relative outlier scores. Paid distribution cannot be ruled out. Clearly irrelevant, non-English and brand-ad results were excluded. No video is guaranteed to go viral. Instagram performance is not inferred from YouTube counts.

## 1. Same AI, better design brief

**Hook:** “Your AI website looks generic? Change this part of the prompt.”

**Original demonstration:** show the finished before/after in the first two seconds, then improve one owned business landing page with a specific typography, spacing and visual-hierarchy brief. Show the actual prompt and the resulting mobile page. Keep it 30–40 seconds, English only, with a small approved presenter overlay that does not hide the screen.

**Thumbnail:** SAME AI. BETTER WEBSITE.

**Resource/CTA concept:** “Comment DESIGN for the brief.” Create and test the resource and automation before using this CTA publicly.

Evidence from three independent creators:
- Sebastian Hardy, [How to build high-end websites for $0 with Claude](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TixdV5DP7oc), 10 October: **162,840 views**.
- Himan Creates, [How to Make 3D websites with Claude and Scrolltide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zNJeSYRH1oc), 30 September: **51,638 views**.
- 100x Engineers, [Make Claude Code build better websites](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TduPBWDRw8s), 4 October: **42,633 views**.

Assessment: strongest triangulation for a practical visual website topic. Generic screenshot-to-code results in the same search were much weaker; avoid treating every website topic as equally promising. Do not copy the unverified “$0” promise.

## 2. One product photo, three ad concepts

**Hook:** “I gave AI one product photo. Here are three ads it could make.”

**Original demonstration:** use an owned product image and build three distinct motion treatments. Open with the best finished shot, then show the inputs and one meaningful editing step. Validate actual output and costs before writing outcome claims. Target 25–35 seconds.

**Thumbnail:** 1 PHOTO → 3 ADS

**Resource/CTA concept:** “Comment AD for the storyboard and prompts.” Proposed resource only.

Evidence of adjacent AI editing interest:
- Runtime Caffeine, [Adobe Is COOKED...](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RwMJSW0OcwY), 7 October: **239,312 views**.
- Nate Herk, [Claude has completely changed how I edit videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CV-k6rPJvJY), 14 October: **84,156 views**.

Assessment: promising test, with fewer independent examples than idea 1. These examples support the broader editing topic, not the exact product-photo format. A specific product transformation distinguishes this from the existing video-editing tutorial and motion-grid reel.

## 3. Three AI agents challenge one business idea

**Hook:** “Before building this business idea, I made three AI agents argue about it.”

**Original demonstration:** give the same brief to a researcher, critic and planner. Show one documented disagreement and how a human checks it. No fabricated mistake, invented revenue or autonomous business claim. Target 35–45 seconds.

**Thumbnail:** 3 AGENTS. 1 IDEA.

**Resource/CTA concept:** “Comment TEAM for the three role prompts.” Proposed resource only.

Evidence across independent creators:
- ReStructure AI, [I Built an Entire Social Media Team With 0 Employees](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JhM-rGP5Kx0), 25 September: **357,772 views**.
- Nate Herk, [I Built the Ultimate AI Board of Advisors](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7GTWAXCTy0o), 13 October: **123,486 views**.
- Eli Sadler, [ECC launches AI agents that design, build, test, and secure apps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYnWlHQ-AgA), 13 October: **181,457 views**.

Assessment: multiple creators support interest in agent workflows, but not specifically an identical three-agent debate. Make the concrete disagreement the payoff instead of a generic list of tools.
