Check before launch

  • One clear primary action; correct destination.
  • The offer says what the business does and for whom.
  • Typography, spacing and palette are consistent.
  • Text remains readable on mobile and when zoomed.
  • No sideways scroll at the tested widths.
  • Links and controls work with a keyboard; focus is visible.
  • Text/background contrast checked, including the button.
  • Images have useful alternative text, or are marked decorative.
  • No fabricated proof, fake customers or unverified outcome claims.
  • A real booking form is connected and tested before launch.

What this demonstration proves

The files show a concrete layout and messaging change. They do not establish higher conversions, revenue, or full accessibility compliance. The sample consultation button scrolls to a disclosure and does not collect information.
