# Third reference: visual analysis

Reference: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DesvNaXM_j5/ · creator @ashutoshwithclaude · approximately 69 seconds. Inspected five contact sheets sampled every 0.5 seconds and the downloaded cover. Source footage stays local and is not an export asset.

The entire reference is a stylized 3D conversation. There is no real filmed human presenter and no verifiable live browser recording. It shows a student in green seated left, an orange floating robot right, a warm wood desk, red carpet, tall cream columns, blue windows and a coffered dome. Popup UI cards illustrate the process. Do not mistake illustrated companies, match scores or application fields for an actual successful run.

## Cover

Downloaded cover is the opening two-shot with rounded outer corners. Most upper space is dome/architecture. Student is in lower-left, robot lower-right, desk at about 72% height. White bold serif subtitle has a dark outline and wraps into two lines across the desk. No arrow or separate headline block. For our cover use Hammad in a casual neutral/green top at a warm desk, an original abstract assistant panel (not the source robot), and the short headline “Prepare your next application.” Keep its warm setting and lower subtitle layout, but crop closer so Hammad is identifiable in a feed.

## Caption system

Bold serif resembling Georgia, white for student lines and yellow/gold for robot lines; dark outline/shadow. Approximately 4–9 words in a clause, 1–2 lines. At the opening captions are around y=74% of height; during popup cards they move to the seam at about y=49–51%. No word-by-word karaoke observed. Color signals speaker, but wording remains intelligible independently. Our version uses legible cream captions with key phrases in amber, dark stroke, at 48–54 px on 1080 width, 3–6 words per chunk. Optional speaker labels if a second voice is actually used; do not impersonate Claude’s voice.

## Rhythm

Four early camera angles in the first fourteen seconds, then a persistent two-shot under five popup panels. The career section swaps four illustrated pages over nine seconds; the form remains for twenty-two seconds while states change. Roughly one significant visual change each 3–5 seconds during the demo, fewer camera cuts thereafter. Character presence continues underneath cards until the final three-second CTA. Our equivalent keeps Hammad visible below the active content, with small crop/punch changes at rhetorical turns and Remotion UI-state animations above him.

## Names seen

Claude, Playwright, Microsoft, LinkedIn, MIT, Google Sheets-style. Career-board labels appear to be illustration data and are not treated as sources. GPA, match percentages, job counts and universal application claims are excluded from our version.
