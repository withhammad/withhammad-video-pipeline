# Voice agent workflow checklist

1. Choose one service and define its successful outcome.
2. Map required identity checks and permissions.
3. Identify the systems of record and their supported APIs.
4. Separate read-only answers from actions that change records.
5. Require explicit confirmation before payments or submissions.
6. Add retries, duplicate protection and a human handoff.
7. Test misheard speech, missing information and failed actions.
8. Map where messages, audio, models and logs are processed; local models alone do not establish end-to-end data residency.
9. Measure completion rate, errors and handoffs before expanding.

Planning resource, not a deployed licensing service.
