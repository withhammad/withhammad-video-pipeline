The seven-row trap

− AED 3,000
Checked · 6 rows
AED 12,000

Order O-1003 appears twice with the same date, currency and amount. CSV row 4 is retained; row 8 is the exact duplicate. In this fictional example, each row is one complete order, so the duplicate is reviewed and removed.

Repeated IDs alone do not prove a mistake. Real orders may span multiple line items. Check the meaning of a row before changing anything.

A review you can reproduce

  1. Preserve the original CSV. State what one row represents.
  2. Confirm AED currency and the 1–6 October 2026 date window.
  3. Flag repeated IDs, compare every field and separate exact duplicates from conflicts.
  4. Review the exact duplicate, then retain six complete orders.
  5. Reconcile AED 15,000 − AED 3,000 = AED 12,000 before creating charts.
