A campaign plan is easier to understand when each decision has a clear place. Start with the outcome you want, then decide who you need to reach and what message they should see. For this example, I am separating four jobs: reaching new customers, testing new creative, reviewing strong performers, and reconnecting with existing customers. These are planning examples, not a guarantee of better results. Keep the labels clear, change one thing at a time when comparing creative, and judge the result against the objective you chose. A neat diagram can help explain the plan, but it cannot replace testing with real data. Build the structure first, then review and improve it.
