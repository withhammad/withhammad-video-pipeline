Your dream could be one of the 100.

We're backing 100 people with a dream to build a creator or media business.

Whether you want to launch a content brand, build the next creator platform, start a production company or reinvent how media works, this is your opportunity to turn an idea into a business.

Applications open 20 November 2026. Register your interest now to be first in line.

What if someone backed your dream?

Imagine having the funding, support and connections to turn your media business idea into reality.

That's what 100 Dreams is about.

We're selecting 100 aspiring entrepreneurs from around the world and helping them take the first steps towards building their own media businesses.

Here's what you'll receive if selected:

USD 10,000 towards your business Funding to help you get your media business off the ground.

A UAE company licence Support to establish your company in the UAE, without needing to relocate.

Golden Visa support Assistance with the Golden Visa process, subject to government approval.

A trip to Dubai Join us at the 1 Billion Followers Summit, taking place 8–10 January 2027.

A 6-week kickstart programme Work with Simon Squibb and the Dream team as you take the first steps towards launching your business.

Who is this for?

Anyone with a dream to build a media business.

We're looking for ideas in:

Content creation and creator-led businesses
Entertainment and digital media
Video, film and media production
Media technology and platforms
Innovative new media business models

Open worldwide.

You don't need to move to Dubai, but you must be able to:

Establish your company in the UAE
Travel to Dubai for the Summit
Participate in the full 6-week programme

You must also be 18 or older.
