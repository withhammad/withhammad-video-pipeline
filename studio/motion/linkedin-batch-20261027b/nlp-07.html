THE NLP FIELD NOTEBOOK

Neural networks

From local patterns to sequence state

6.1

Feedforward networks

Transform fixed-size features through learned layers. They are useful building blocks even inside large transformers.

6.2

Recurrent networks

Process a sequence through a changing hidden state. Long dependencies can be difficult to learn with simple recurrence.

6.3

LSTM and GRU

Gates regulate which information to retain or update. These architectures help manage sequence information across steps.

6.4

Practical tradeoffs

Choose based on latency, data size, sequence length and hardware. A newer architecture is not automatically the best fit for a small task.
