5. Language models

Predict a distribution over what comes next

5.1  The objective

•  A causal language model estimates the next token from the preceding tokens.

•  A sequence probability is built from these conditional predictions.

5.2  N-gram models

•  Condition on a short fixed history.

•  Smoothing prevents unseen combinations from receiving zero probability.

5.3  Neural models

•  Learn reusable representations instead of keeping only explicit count tables.

•  The architecture determines how context is processed.

5.4  Perplexity

•  Measures how surprising held-out text is to the model.

•  Compare scores only under compatible tokenization and evaluation conditions.

P(sequence) = product of next-token probabilities
