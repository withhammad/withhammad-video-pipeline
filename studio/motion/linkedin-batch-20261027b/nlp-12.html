11. NLP applications

The same text can support different tasks

11.1  Classification and routing

•  Map a message to a category or intent.

•  Track per-class errors and the consequences of a wrong label.

11.2  Entity extraction

•  Find names, dates or domain-specific fields.

•  Evaluate boundaries as well as the extracted values.

11.3  Translation and summaries

•  Preserve meaning and important qualifications.

•  A fluent answer may still omit or invent details.

11.4  Question answering

•  Separate the ability to retrieve relevant evidence from the ability to answer accurately using it.

Choose the task before choosing the model.
