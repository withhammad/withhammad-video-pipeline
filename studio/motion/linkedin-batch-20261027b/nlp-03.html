2. Text preprocessing

Keep the information the task needs

2.1  Tokenization

•  Split text using the tokenizer expected by your model.

•  Words, subwords and bytes are different choices; token boundaries are model-dependent.

2.2  Normalize with care

•  Whitespace cleanup can help.

•  Lowercasing, punctuation removal and stop-word removal may erase useful meaning or violate a pretrained model’s input conventions.

2.3  Stems and lemmas

•  Stemming uses surface rules; lemmatization aims for dictionary forms.

•  Neither is a mandatory step for transformer models.

2.4  Avoid leakage

•  Fit vocabulary and preprocessing statistics on training data only.

•  Apply the same saved pipeline to validation and test data.
