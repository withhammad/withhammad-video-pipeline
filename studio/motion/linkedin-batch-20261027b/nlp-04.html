3. Representing text

Choose what similarity should mean

3.1  Bag of words

•  Count vocabulary items.

•  It is inexpensive and interpretable, but ignores word order unless you add n-grams.

3.2  TF-IDF

•  Increase the weight of terms distinctive to a document and reduce the influence of terms common across the collection.

3.3  N-grams

•  Adjacent sequences preserve some local order.

•  “Not useful” can behave differently from either word alone.

3.4  Dense vectors

•  Learn continuous representations that can capture semantic relationships.

•  Their quality depends on the training objective and the domain.
