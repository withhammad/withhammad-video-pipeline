THE NLP FIELD NOTEBOOK

Word embeddings

Useful geometry, imperfect meaning

4.1

Static embeddings

Word2Vec, GloVe and FastText learn reusable word representations. A static vector does not adapt to each new sentence.

4.2

Training intuition

CBOW predicts a target from nearby words; skip-gram predicts nearby words from a target. These objectives learn distributional patterns.

4.3

Subword information

FastText represents words through character n-grams, which can help with morphology and uncommon forms.

4.4

Similarity

Cosine similarity compares vector directions. A high score is a model signal, not proof that two statements mean the same thing.

“bank” by a river ≠ “bank” holding a deposit
