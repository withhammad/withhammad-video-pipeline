# The 16 scene lines

One object, one change. Each tile = style.md + brief.md + one of these lines (the brief's last line).

01. **Particles → logo:** Particles hold a diamond mark, burst into a slow ring, and snap back.  
    File: tiles/01-particles-logo.html

02. **Gradient → flow:** The loud gradient under a slanted edge, its colour bands drifting like liquid.  
    File: tiles/02-gradient-flow.html

03. **Glass → tilt:** A frosted glass card over two colour glows tilts in 3D while one sweep of light crosses it.  
    File: tiles/03-glass-tilt.html

04. **Globe → arcs:** A dotted globe turns once per loop while payment arcs fly out of Dubai.  
    File: tiles/04-globe-arcs.html

05. **Line → draw:** A revenue card counts up while its line draws in left to right, then a +128% badge pops.  
    File: tiles/05-line-draw.html

06. **Shape → morph:** One gradient shape melts from circle to square to star to blob and back, its name above.  
    File: tiles/06-shape-morph.html

07. **Grid → wave:** A field of dots in perspective rolls like a sheet of silk.  
    File: tiles/07-grid-wave.html

08. **Cards → shuffle:** Five payment cards in a fanned deck; the front card flicks out and slides to the back.  
    File: tiles/08-cards-shuffle.html

09. **Number → roll:** A revenue figure rolls up digit by digit like an odometer while the bars grow under it.  
    File: tiles/09-number-roll.html

10. **Ribbon → twist:** A ribbon of 70 hairlines twists through itself, orange on the left to violet on the right.  
    File: tiles/10-ribbon-twist.html

11. **Liquid → merge:** Three glossy purple drops drift together, merge into one, then pull apart.  
    File: tiles/11-liquid-merge.html

12. **Phone → explode:** An isometric phone lifts its screen, card, list and button apart into layers, then snaps shut.  
    File: tiles/12-phone-explode.html

13. **Rings → orbit:** A glowing planet with tilted rings; three moons orbit at different speeds, passing behind it.  
    File: tiles/13-rings-orbit.html

14. **Words → kinetic:** Three heavy words slide up one at a time, the last in the gradient; then the next phrase.  
    File: tiles/14-words-kinetic.html

15. **Button → pay:** A cursor clicks Pay, the button shrinks to a spinner, ticks green and opens into Paid.  
    File: tiles/15-button-pay.html

16. **Confetti → burst:** A gradient tick pops and confetti bursts out, tumbles and falls away.  
    File: tiles/16-confetti-burst.html
