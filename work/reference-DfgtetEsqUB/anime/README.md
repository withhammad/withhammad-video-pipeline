# Personal anime walking revision

Owner explicitly requested a cartoon of himself walking through outdoor scenes and valleys, using Blender or code. Personal use only; do not register it in the business publishing pipeline.

Built-in imagegen supplied the original anime illustration assets; the saved Hammad photo is identity reference. Blender 5.2.3 LTS drives a 17-bone full-body mesh, with independently keyed legs, knees, feet, counter-swinging arms, spine, head, blinking and hair. This is layered 2D character animation, not a photorealistic avatar or a newly filmed walk. The Blender PNG sequence is composited on painted terrace, valley, river and courtyard scenes in Remotion.

Original supplied audio and its exact timing remain unchanged. Arabic, Roman Urdu and English captions remain. No HeyGen or paid generation credits. No public posting authorization.
