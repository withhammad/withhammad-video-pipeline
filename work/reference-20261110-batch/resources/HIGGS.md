# Make videos inside Claude with Higgsfield

Checked 10 November 2026. Keyword: **HIGGS**.

## Connect the tools

1. Open [Higgsfield's official MCP page](https://higgsfield.ai/mcp) and use its current connection instructions.
2. In Claude, open **Settings → Connectors**. Find Higgsfield, or use the official custom-connector URL shown by
   Higgsfield if the directory option is unavailable.
3. Sign in to your own Higgsfield account and review the requested permissions. Return to a Claude conversation
   with the connector available.
4. Describe one short shot: the subject, action, camera movement, style, duration and aspect ratio.
5. Ask Claude to show the selected model and quoted credit cost before generating. Inspect the returned clip
   before asking for a second generation.

Example brief:

> Create a vertical product shot of my supplied coffee cup on a sunlit desk. A slow camera push-in, subtle steam,
> natural shadows, five seconds. Keep the logo and product shape unchanged. Show the selected model and credit
> cost before generating; wait for my confirmation.

## About the launch offer

Higgsfield's homepage currently advertises Fable 5.2 × Higgsfield MCP unlimited generations for new users. The
specific account eligibility, qualifying models, dates and limits must be read in the live offer. This guide does
not promise three days, all models, no payment details, or permanent free generation. Other Higgsfield unlimited
plans and MCP credit terms may differ. Check the cost shown immediately before a generation.

## Review before publishing

Check readable text, correct product identity, coherent motion, audio rights and platform disclosures. Keep the
original generation and note the settings so a targeted fix does not become an unnecessary full rebuild.
