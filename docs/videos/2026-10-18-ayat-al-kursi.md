## Draft caption (for the separate account; edit freely)
```
Which verse in the Book of Allah is the greatest? The Prophet ﷺ asked Ubayy ibn Kaʿb twice. The second time, Ubayy
recited Ayat al-Kursi, and the Prophet ﷺ struck his chest and said: "May knowledge be pleasant for you, O
Abu'l-Mundhir!" (Sahih Muslim 810)

Read it line by line and the lines pair up: the first with the last, the second with the eighth, the third with the
seventh, the fourth with the sixth. One line stands alone in the middle: He knows what is before them and what is
behind them.

The mushaf's pause marks give nine lines. Ibn Kathir counted ten sentences, splitting the first in two. Either way,
the centre is the same.

Translation: M. Pickthall (1930). Ring reading: R. Farrin (2014), M. Azaiez (2013).

```
